### 描述

大目标不一定要靠一次爆发完成。懒人不懒帮你把目标拆成真正能开始的最小行动，用清晰的今日清单、打卡反馈和适时提醒，把“偶尔努力”变成“持续坚持”。

你可以：

- 创建多个独立目标，设置每日、每周固定日或每周次数计划；
- 为每个目标记录完成或保底完成，状态不好时也不中断节奏；
- 在首页查看今天待完成和已完成的目标；
- 查看连续完成、持续坚持、本周进度和累计打卡；
- 设置普通通知与截止前监督提醒；
- 自由切换多套颜色主题。

在 iOS 26 及以上版本，你还可以使用闹钟式监督提醒、专注倒计时，以及锁屏、灵动岛和待机显示中的实时活动。iOS 17–25 使用普通通知提醒，不支持上述 AlarmKit 与实时活动能力。

所有目标和打卡记录均保存在本机。无需注册账号，无广告，无内购，也不依赖云端服务。

## English (U.S.)

Small Steps, Lasting Progress

### Description

Big goals do not have to begin with a burst of motivation. LazyButNot helps you turn goals into small actions you can actually start, then stay consistent with a clear daily list, flexible check-ins, and timely reminders.

You can:

- Create multiple goals with daily, fixed-weekday, or weekly-count schedules.
- Record a full or minimum check-in so a difficult day does not break your rhythm.
- See pending and completed goals together on the Today screen.
- Review completion streaks, consistency, weekly progress, and total check-ins.
- Schedule standard notifications and supervision reminders before deadlines.
- Personalize the app with multiple color themes.

On iOS 26 and later, you can also use alarm-style supervision reminders, focus countdowns, and Live Activities on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and StandBy. On iOS 17–25, reminders use standard notifications and the AlarmKit and Live Activity features above are unavailable.

Goals and check-in records stay on your device. No account, ads, in-app purchases, or cloud service is required.

## App Review Notes

LazyButNot does not require sign-in. Goals, check-in records, app settings, and active focus-session state are stored locally on the device. The app does not use a server, advertising, analytics, or third-party data collection SDKs.

Notification permission is requested for goal reminders. Creating goals and recording check-ins remain available when notification permission is denied.

On iOS 26 and later, AlarmKit permission enables alarm-style supervision reminders and focus countdowns. To test a countdown: open the Today tab, tap the focus entry in the summary card, choose a short duration, and tap Start. The app then opens the focus detail screen, and its Live Activity can appear on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and StandBy. The active countdown can be cancelled from the focus detail screen.

On iOS 17–25, the app uses the standard UserNotifications compatibility path and does not expose AlarmKit countdown functionality.
