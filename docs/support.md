# 懒人不懒技术支持 / LazyButNot Support

## 中文

“懒人不懒”是一款本地目标坚持 App，支持目标管理、每日打卡、普通通知提醒，以及 iOS 26 及以上版本的闹钟式监督提醒、专注倒计时和实时活动。

### 快速使用

1. 在“目标”页创建目标，并设置周期、提醒时间和最小行动。
2. 在“今日”页查看待完成目标，选择“完成”或“保底完成”进行打卡。
3. 如需提醒，在“设置”页允许通知权限；iOS 26 及以上版本还可允许闹钟权限。
4. 首页的专注入口可创建倒计时；iOS 26 及以上版本可在锁屏、灵动岛和待机显示中查看进度。

### 权限说明

- 通知权限：发送目标提醒和截止前监督提醒。
- 闹钟权限（iOS 26 及以上）：提供闹钟式监督提醒和专注倒计时。
- 拒绝权限不会影响目标创建和打卡，相关提醒功能将不可用或降级。

## English

LazyButNot is a local goal consistency app with goal management, daily check-ins, standard notification reminders, and—on iOS 26 and later—alarm-style supervision reminders, focus countdowns, and Live Activities.

### Quick Start

1. Create a goal on the Goals tab and set its schedule, reminder time, and minimum action.
2. Review pending goals on the Today tab and choose Completed or Minimum Completed to check in.
3. To receive reminders, allow notifications in Settings. On iOS 26 and later, you can also allow alarm access.
4. Use the focus entry on the home screen to start a countdown. On iOS 26 and later, progress can appear on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and StandBy.

### Permissions

- Notifications: deliver goal reminders and supervision reminders before deadlines.
- Alarms (iOS 26 and later): provide alarm-style supervision reminders and focus countdowns.
- Denying permissions does not affect goal creation or check-ins, but related reminder features will be unavailable or reduced.
