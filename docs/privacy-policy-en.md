# LazyButNot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Effective date: October 17, 2026

LazyButNot (the “App”) is a goal consistency app that does not require an account and primarily stores data locally. This policy explains how the App handles data and uses system permissions.

## 1. Data We Handle

The App lets you create goals, record check-ins, select a theme, and start focus countdowns. This content is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to a developer server.

The current version:

- does not require registration or an account;
- does not use advertising or third-party analytics services;
- does not collect, sell, or share personal data;
- does not track you across apps or websites; and
- does not include third-party data collection SDKs.

## 2. Local Storage, System Backups, and Deletion

Goals, check-in records, theme settings, and active focus sessions are stored in the App's local storage.

- Deleting a goal also deletes that goal and its history.
- Uninstalling the App causes iOS to remove the App's local data.
- The current version does not provide developer-server sync or backups.
- If you enable device or iCloud backups provided by Apple, a system backup may include the App's local data. That processing is controlled by Apple's services and your system settings.

## 3. System Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

- Notifications: to deliver goal reminders and supervision reminders before a deadline.
- Alarms (iOS 26 and later): to provide alarm-style supervision reminders and focus countdowns.
- Live Activities (iOS 26 and later): to display focus countdown status on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and StandBy.

Denying these permissions does not prevent you from creating goals or recording check-ins, but the related reminders or displays may be unavailable. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## 4. Device Diagnostics

Whether device diagnostics are shared with Apple is controlled by your iOS privacy and analytics settings. The developer does not actively collect this information through the App.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not actively collect personal data from any user and does not profile, advertise to, or track children.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If the App's data-handling practices change, we will update the date and content of this policy and, when applicable, notify you through the App or a public page.
