简体中文

“懒人不懒”（以下简称“本 App”）是一款无需账号、以本地存储为主的目标坚持应用。本政策说明本 App 如何处理数据及使用系统权限。

1. 我们处理的数据

本 App 允许你创建目标、记录打卡、选择主题并启动专注倒计时。这些内容由本 App 保存在你的设备上，不会上传到开发者服务器。

  • 不要求注册或登录账号；
  • 不接入广告或第三方分析服务；
  • 不收集、出售或共享个人数据；
  • 不进行跨 App 或跨网站跟踪；
  • 不包含第三方数据收集 SDK。

2. 本地存储、系统备份与删除

目标、打卡记录、主题设置和活动中的专注会话保存在 App 的本地存储中。本 App 不提供开发者服务器同步或备份。

  • 删除目标时，该目标及其历史记录会一并删除；
  • 卸载本 App 时，iOS 会移除属于本 App 的本地数据；
  • 如果你启用了 Apple 提供的设备或 iCloud 备份，系统备份可能包含本 App 的本地数据，其处理受 Apple 服务和你的系统设置控制。

3. 系统权限

  • 通知权限：用于在你设置的时间发送目标提醒和截止前监督提醒；
  • 闹钟权限（iOS 26 及以上）：用于提供闹钟式监督提醒和专注倒计时；
  • 实时活动（iOS 26 及以上）：用于在锁屏、灵动岛和待机显示中展示专注倒计时状态。

拒绝上述权限不会阻止你创建目标或进行打卡，但对应的提醒或展示功能可能不可用。你可以随时在 iOS“设置”中修改权限。

4. 设备诊断

设备诊断信息是否与 Apple 共享，由你的 iOS 隐私与分析设置决定。开发者不会通过本 App 主动收集这些信息。

5. 儿童隐私

本 App 不会主动收集任何用户的个人数据，也不会针对儿童进行数据画像、广告投放或跟踪。

6. 政策更新

如果本 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策的日期和内容，并在适用时通过 App 或本页面告知你。

7. 联系方式

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请发送邮件至 hunao163@gmail.com。你主动发送邮件时，邮箱地址、邮件内容以及你选择附带的设备信息或截图会由邮件服务商传递，并仅用于回复和处理你的请求；本 App 不会自动读取或上传这些信息。

English

LazyButNot (the “App”) is a goal consistency app that does not require an account and primarily stores data locally. This policy explains how the App handles data and uses system permissions.

1. Data We Handle

The App lets you create goals, record check-ins, select a theme, and start focus countdowns. The App stores this content on your device and does not upload it to a developer server.

  • No registration or account is required.
  • The App does not use advertising or third-party analytics services.
  • The App does not collect, sell, or share personal data.
  • The App does not track you across apps or websites.
  • The App does not include third-party data collection SDKs.

2. Local Storage, System Backups, and Deletion

Goals, check-in records, theme settings, and active focus sessions are stored in the App's local storage. The App does not provide developer-server sync or backups.

  • Deleting a goal also deletes that goal and its history.
  • Uninstalling the App causes iOS to remove the App's local data.
  • If you enable device or iCloud backups provided by Apple, a system backup may include the App's local data. That processing is controlled by Apple's services and your system settings.

3. System Permissions

  • Notifications: deliver goal reminders and supervision reminders before deadlines.
  • Alarms (iOS 26 and later): provide alarm-style supervision reminders and focus countdowns.
  • Live Activities (iOS 26 and later): display focus countdown status on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and StandBy.

Denying these permissions does not prevent you from creating goals or recording check-ins, but the related reminders or displays may be unavailable. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Device Diagnostics

Whether device diagnostics are shared with Apple is controlled by your iOS privacy and analytics settings. The developer does not actively collect this information through the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not actively collect personal data from any user and does not profile, advertise to, or track children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If the App's data-handling practices change, we will update the date and content of this policy and, when applicable, notify you through the App or this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, email hunao163@gmail.com. When you choose to send an email, your address, message, and any device information or screenshots you attach are transmitted by your email provider and used only to reply to and handle your request. The App does not automatically read or upload this information.
